ROBUSTNESS CHECK: methodology-encoding x autonomy under independent elicitation
==============================================================================

A complementary check on the released annotation, which stands as published.
It re-elicits two labels with the two dimensions separated and asks whether the
association reported in Section 5.3 moves.

SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------
  A supplementary robustness check, not a revalidation of the dataset. Both
  conditions read the same README-derived summary, so this separates
  joint-elicitation coupling from corpus structure and nothing more. It does not
  test whether either label is CORRECT; the human pass in 5.human-anno does that,
  and coder B's blind sheet (coding_sheet_coderB.csv) is still unfilled.
  `gpt-4o-mini` is a rolling alias; the `released` row may have been produced by
  an earlier snapshot, which is why `joint` is re-run here as the true control.

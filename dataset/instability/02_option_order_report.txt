SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A supplementary robustness check, not a revalidation of the dataset. It
  measures whether the label is STABLE under presentation order, not whether it
  is RIGHT. Four orders that agree can agree on a wrong label.
  Only the human pass separates the two, and coder B's blind sheet
  (5.human-anno/coding_sheet_coderB.csv) is still unfilled.
  Methodology encoding is binary and is not tested here; a two-option list has
  a position effect too, and 01_decoupled_annotation.py is the place it would
  show up as a marginal shift.
